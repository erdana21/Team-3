
Software Requirements Specification (SRS) for CampusRide
1. Introduction
CampusRide is a shared mobility platform designed for university students to share rides to campus buildings, reducing travel costs and traffic congestion.
2. User Roles
 * Driver: Offers rides, sets routes, schedules, and pricing.
 * Passenger: Searches for rides, books seats, and shares travel costs.
 * Admin: Manages user accounts, platform monitoring, and dispute resolution.
3. Functional Requirements
 * Authentication: Users must log in via university email.
 * Ride Posting: Drivers can create trips specifying origin, destination, departure time, and available seats.
